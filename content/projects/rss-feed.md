<p>Mouser is a macOS desktop app for reading RSS, Atom, Substack and YouTube feeds.</p>

                <p>Articles cached in SQLite so the app can load recent content immediately, support pagination and search, and continue displaying previously downloaded items offline. Feeds refresh concurrently in the background and YouTube videos can be played directly inside the app.</p>

                <p>I will be making <a href="https://github.com/gregorycotton/mouser" target="_blank" class="primary-link">updates to this project</a> as my personal needs evolve.</p>
