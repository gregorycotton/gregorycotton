<p>Web portfolio for ANVIL, a directing duo from Toronto, Ontario.</p>

                <p>Fun highlights from this project include:</p>

                <br>

                <ul style="list-style-position: inside;">
                    <li>We were very inspired by a raw early-2000s web aesthetic, and a lot of <a href="https://www.are.na/charles-broskoski/scientist-websites" target="_blank" class="primary-link">scientist websites</a> but wanted to mix this aesthetic with something more modern and on brand for ANVIL.</li>
                    <li>I had a dream where Toby asked me for a dark mode on the website. I built the feature (inverting colours, as is typical on a lot of modern websites), and he told me he meant that he ‘wanted the lights off’. The next day, I shared this dream with Toby over FaceTime and we decided it was actually a funny enough play on 'dark mode' to implement on the site you see today. </li>
                </ul>

                <br>

                <p>Co-creative directed alongside Toby and Tank. Developed by me. Additional graphics by me as well.</p>

                <br>

                <p>Visit the site at <a href="https://anvil.cool/" target="_blank" class="primary-link">anvil.cool</a>.</p>
