For the entirety of February 2024, I disabled all of my adblockers across all devices, and reported and cataloged every online advertisement I saw. I purposefully negatively impacted my experience online by forcing myself to engage and be attentive to the content I normally would ignore.

This include digital banners, overlays, popups, etc. on webpages, advertisements before/during videos on streaming services, sponsorships/advertisements inserted into content (e.g a blog post with a sponsored paragraph, an ad read on a YouTube video, a sports team with an ‘official beer’, etc.), commercials on television, spam emails, and more.

I reported 2,056 advertisements.
